Privacy Policy

WheelsEye Truck Branding app · last updated November 2026

This app is used by WheelsEye Technology and its branding vendors to record truck sticker pasting work. It is only for people who have been given a personal link by WheelsEye.

What we collect

  • Your email address — the one WheelsEye registered for you. If Google sign-in is turned on for your link, Google shares your email address with us so we can check that the link is being used by you. We do not get your Google password.
  • Work entries — sticker type, vehicle number, the photo (and optional video) you take, and the date and time.
  • Location — the GPS position of your phone when you take the photo, and the city, state and pincode for that position.

How we use it

Only to verify sticker pasting work, prepare reports for WheelsEye and your vendor, check for duplicate entries, and calculate work done. We do not sell this information and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Entries are stored in WheelsEye's Google Sheets and Google Drive. They can be seen by authorised WheelsEye staff and by your vendor (for its own pasters only). Photos can be opened by anyone who has the photo's link, so these links are shared only inside WheelsEye's tools.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for the branding programme, payments and audits, after which it is archived or deleted.

Your choices

You can stop using your link at any time. To ask about or remove your data, contact us below.
